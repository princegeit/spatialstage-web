Looking for the helper on this PC...

A plain song (an MP3, or a WAV of the full mix) plays as one sound. To move the vocals, drums, bass, guitar and piano around you separately, it has to be split into stems first. SpatialStage Helper does that on your own PC with Demucs - your songs are never uploaded.

  1. Download the helper Windows 10/11, 64-bit · Mac and Linux not yet
  2. Extract the zip and double-click Install SpatialStage Helper.bat. It downloads about 1 GB (Python, PyTorch, Demucs and the model) - 5 to 15 minutes, no admin rights needed.
  3. Come back here: it connects by itself. If the browser asks to look for and connect to devices on your local network, choose Allow - that is this page talking to the helper on your PC.
Already installed?

Blocked the browser's question by mistake? Click the icon at the left of the address bar and allow Local network access for this site, then Check again.
